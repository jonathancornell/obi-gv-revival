# Sources and further reading

Where to go for more information, other guides, and help. Links were checked on 2026-11-22. Third-party content belongs to its authors; see [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md).

## Firmware and the original project
- **obifirmware.com**: https://www.obifirmware.com/ — the community firmware downloads (including OBi100/110, OBi1000-series, OBi5xx, OBi2000 and VVX D230, not covered here), per-model install order, MD5 values, change logs, and the Google Voice provisioning files. It also links archives of the original DSLReports threads, which are the best technical history of how the firmware was built.

## Step-by-step guides by other people
- **OBi2xx/3xx guide (Google Doc)**: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_6k0-z18mzHbj0y_vmph_yk5b-FCqqaPNSScAQU5wjM/edit — illustrated walkthrough using a browser; very careful about power-cycling and Chrome.
- **OBi10xx guide (Google Doc)**: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TTWPpZ8BVYzRjNLHEKEf50IPmPyaOah1XMJZAIhgzQw/edit — for OBi1022/1032/1062 phones.
- **OBi21xx guide (Google Doc)**: https://docs.google.com/document/d/142HOI5pqUHc-6qCbLTm2twVDe-80PopUZIAMVXlIz2U/edit — for OBi2182 and relatives.
- The announcement thread for those guides: https://broadbandbulletin.com/d/2557-firmware-upgrade-guides-obihai-2xx3xx10xx21xx

## Community forums (best place to ask questions)
- **Broadband Bulletin, VoIP Tech Chat**: https://broadbandbulletin.com/t/voip-tech-chat — the current home of the community that built and supports the firmware, including its author. Search before posting.
- **Firmware mods thread**: https://broadbandbulletin.com/d/73-obihai-obi20x30x-obi1000-obi50x-obi2000-firmware-mods — ongoing discussion, release announcements and fixes for the 20x/30x family.
- **"Obihai and GV"**: https://broadbandbulletin.com/d/341-obihai-and-gv — Google Voice status, certificate expiry and token reports.
- **RedFlagDeals OBiTALK end-of-life thread**: https://forums.redflagdeals.com/end-life-notice-obitalk-october-31st-2024-affects-consumer-google-voice-obihai-atas-ip-phones-2710267/ — user discussion around the OBiTALK shutdown (may need a normal browser to load).

## Other copies and background
- **Kavex's OBi200 firmware archive on GitHub**: https://github.com/Kavex/OBi200 — a personal mirror of earlier firmware and install notes.
- **Obihai Technology (Wikipedia)**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obihai_Technology — company history (Obihai, Polycom, Plantronics, HP).

## Google account pages used in the guide
- **Manage connections to Google Voice**: https://myaccount.google.com/connections — remove old Google Voice grants before provisioning.

## Related, if this route does not fit
- Porting your number to an independent SIP provider and using a SIP-capable adapter avoids the dependency on Google's unofficial path altogether. The forum threads above discuss providers people moved to.
